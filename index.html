SPECIFICATION PROTOCOLS & INQUIRIES [ 05 ENTRIES // INDEXED ]
01 HOW DO I CHOOSE THE RIGHT SIZE?

Take all our items to normal reality according to your body measurements before ordering. All pieces are tailored with an architectural drop-shoulder cut designed for fluid layering. We recommend selecting your true size for the intended contemporary drape, or sizing down for an aerodynamic technical fit. Consult our calibrated biometric sizing matrix for detailed chest, sleeve, and hem metrics.

02 WHAT MATERIALS ARE USED IN THE JACKET?

Engineered from a 4-tier composite lamination: Japanese high-density DWR Ripstop exterior, sub-micron breathable micro-porous membrane (28,000mm hydrostatic head / 20,000 g/m² breathability), space-grade Aerogel Matrix V-9 synthetic baffles (-25°C arctic rating), and titanium-infused radiant heat-reflective interior lining.

03 HOW SHOULD I CARE FOR MY JACKET?

Machine wash on delicate cold cycle (30°C / 86°F) with technical outer-wear detergent. Do not bleach or use fabric softeners. Tumble dry on low heat for 20 minutes to reactivate the hydrophobic DWR surface tension, or line dry away from direct sunlight. Never dry clean or iron.

04 DO YOU SHIP INTERNATIONALLY?

Yes. Complimentary priority global dispatch via DHL Express Carbon-Neutral with signature delivery. Domestic transit: 1–2 business days. International transit: 3–5 business days. Each piece is dispatched inside a sealed climate-controlled archival flight case with individual cryptographic NFC serialization.

05 CAN I RETURN OR EXCHANGE MY ORDER?

We offer a 14-day complimentary trial period from the delivery confirmation date. Items must remain unwashed, unworn, and accompanied by the original tactical garment case, serialized authentication certificate, and untampered cryptographic security seals.
